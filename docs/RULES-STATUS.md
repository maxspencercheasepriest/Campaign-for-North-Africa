# Rules implementation status

This checklist tracks which parts of the rules are in the game. Section numbers follow the original rulebook, so you can look each one up. Our code and wording are our own.

Legend: ✅ implemented · 🟡 partly or simplified · ⬜ not started · 🔶 placeholder (stand-in rules until the real ones are written)

## Notes on the charts

Every column of every dice table is checked automatically to cover all 36 results exactly once (`test/tables.test.js`). That check turned up four printed entries in the scan that can't be right. Each has been corrected to the only reading that fits its column, and each correction is commented in `src/shared/tables.js`:

- Close Assault, attacker, column −2, 20% losses: printed 13-18, read as 13-16.
- Close Assault, defender, column +2, 5% losses: printed 41-52, read as 34-52.
- Close Assault, defender, column +4, 10% losses: printed 24-45, read as 34-45.
- Morale Modifier, cohesion −4, −2 column: printed 42-55, read as 42-56.

## Data still to enter

- **Map:** 🟡 all five sections are digitised automatically by `tools/map/` (OCR of hex numbers → grid fit → colour classification of each hex and hexside). That gives about 7,000 hexes and about 2,000 hexside features, and the game uses the board's hex numbers (Tobruk = C4807). The coast road is traced between the towns it runs through (`tools/map/coastroad.py`). Known weak spots: hexes split between two terrains take whichever covers the centre; villages, oases, airfields and minefields aren't read yet. Fixes go in `src/shared/mapfixes.js`.

## House rules (not in the book)

- **Port rewards** (lobby option, on by default): each Game-Turn a side earns 3, 2 or 1 points for every big, medium or small port it took from the other side and still holds. Every 10 points pulls that side's next scheduled reinforcement forward to its best captured port. Points are shown in the Supply tab.
- **U-boats** (part of the Mediterranean Fleet option): three Axis boats arrive in the autumn of 1941 (Game-Turn 100 and 104). The Axis names a patrol sea hex each stage; a boat finds a ship within one hex on a 5-6, torpedoes the biggest on a 4-6 (3 Gun points off a battleship or heavy cruiser, 2 off others), then is sunk if a die roll is no higher than the number of destroyers within two hexes (max 5). It rests two stages after patrol.

## Engineers and construction (rules 23-24)
Built: fortifications (new, Level 1 or 2, and rebuilt city levels), road rebuilding, railway repair; engineer, infantry (3+ TOE) and idle-unit requirements; work counts only for an Operations Stage in which the party spends no CP, is not pinned and has no storm; Stores paid at the start. Fortifications (built or city) shift the defender's column and can be bombed; only major cities ignore retreat results.
Stand-ins: (1) Engineer counters are not in our data yet, so each side starts with invented stand-ins (Axis: two Italian engineer battalions; Commonwealth: two engineer battalions and the SA road coy), plus the NZ railway coys and the 200th Engineer Bn from the reinforcement schedule. Send real counters and I will replace them. (2) Stores are one pool per side (300 each, +25 per Game-Turn) and a supply unit must stand in the hex.
Not built: new railway track [24.62, 24.67], unfinished Via Balbia stretches [24.51], minefields, air-facility and repair-facility construction, hot-weather water [24.21], engineer assault bonus [23.24].

## SAS Brigade (27.8)
Built: arrives GT47 (campaign game), raids an Axis airfield or landing strip it stands in (3 CP, no enemy combat units in the hex, once a month and six stages apart), destroys planes on the ground, flying boats unaffected. Stand-in: the SAS Raid Table [27.93] is not in our scan, so the die results are invented (1-2 nothing, 3 10%, 4 25%, 5 33%, 6 50% of each type).
